How to play

Chromaclash is simple to pick up: keep matching, keep shedding, and remember to shout on your last card. These are the house rules, written for this game.

The goal

Be the first player with an empty hand. The round ends the moment someone plays their final card, and everyone deals a fresh round.

The 112-card well

Four hues — Ember ▲, Tide ●, Moss ■ and Sun ◆ — each carry one 0, two of every 1–9, and the specials below. Eight Prism cards have no hue of their own. Every card also shows its hue's shape, so the table is readable without relying on colour alone.

On your turn

Play one card that shares the current hue or the same number or special as the top card. No match? Draw one from the well. If the card you drew fits, you may play it immediately; otherwise you keep it and the turn passes. A Prism card fits on anything.

Specials

  • ⊘Jam — the next player loses their turn.
  • ⟲Loop — play reverses direction. With two players it comes straight back to you.
  • +2Toll — the next player takes two cards and sits the turn out.
  • ⇄Swap — take any rival's hand and hand them yours.
  • ✦Prism — name the hue that must be matched next.
  • ✦4Prism Toll — name a hue; the next player draws four and sits out. Playable at any time.

SOLO!

When you play down to one card, you must call SOLO before the table catches you. Hit the SOLO button while it is lit, or answer the prompt in time. Miss it and you draw two cards. Rivals have to call too — if one is slow, hit Catch.

Rooms, codes & chat

Host a room to get a 5-character code and an invite link. Friends open the link or type the code, join the lobby with a name, and chat while they wait. The host adds smart bots to fill empty seats and deals when everyone is ready. The host's tab runs the table, so keep it open — if a guest drops mid-round, their seat is held for 60 seconds so they can reconnect; after that a bot takes over their hand.

An empty well

If the face-down well runs dry, the played pile is shuffled back into it, keeping the top card in place. Drawing still works, and the round keeps going.

House rules

You lead the first round in solo; in a room the host leads. Prism Toll can be played on any turn. Rounds are independent — solo carries your win count in this browser, and a room carries wins for as long as the room stays open.
